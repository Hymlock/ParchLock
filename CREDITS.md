# ParchLock Credits

Adapted by Hymlock Groves. Based on Parchmap by Roy Lazarovich.

The storybook icon artwork was generated with OpenAI image generation, directed
and selected by Hymlock Groves. Its four left-page scenes use the project's
opening splash artwork. Codex prepared the platform icon files; Claude reviewed
their integration.

ParchLock uses Parchment and its interactive-fiction interpreter ecosystem,
including Quixe, ZVM, Emglken, Bocfel, Glulxe and AsyncGlk. It also uses jQuery,
jQuery slimScroll, Capacitor, the Capacitor Community speech-recognition plugin,
and Roboto Mono.

Project and dependency licenses remain with their respective authors. See
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `NOTICE`, and the license files in the
corresponding source archive.

Splash artwork provenance and release rights must be recorded before a public
release. No ownership of Parchmap, Parchment, interpreters, or user-supplied
story modules is claimed.
