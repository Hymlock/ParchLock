# ParchLock Credits

Adapted by Hymlock Groves. Based on Parchmap by Roy Lazarovich.

ParchLock was developed by Hymlock Groves with AI assistance from OpenAI Codex,
Anthropic Claude and xAI Grok, which helped with research, code, testing,
reviews and documentation at Hymlock Groves' direction.

The opening splash artwork was generated by Hymlock Groves with AI image
generation. The storybook icon artwork was generated with OpenAI image
generation, directed and selected by Hymlock Groves; its four left-page scenes
reuse the splash artwork. Codex prepared the platform icon files; Claude
reviewed their integration.

ParchLock uses Parchment and its interactive-fiction interpreter ecosystem,
including Quixe, ZVM, Emglken, Bocfel, Glulxe and AsyncGlk. It also uses jQuery,
jQuery slimScroll, Capacitor (with its App, Filesystem and Share plugins), the
Capacitor Community speech-recognition and text-to-speech plugins, and Roboto
Mono.

Project and dependency licenses remain with their respective authors. See
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `NOTICE`, and the license files in the
corresponding source archive.

Artwork provenance (confirmed by Hymlock Groves, 2026-11-07): the splash and
storybook artwork, like the rest of ParchLock's own additions, was created by
Hymlock Groves with AI assistance and his own design decisions. No outside
artwork or assets were used, apart from the original Parchmap and Parchment
projects credited above. No ownership of Parchmap, Parchment,
interpreters, or user-supplied or included story modules is claimed.
