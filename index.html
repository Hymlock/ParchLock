Follow the story.
Keep your bearings.

Choose a story from your library, or open Modules to add a compatible file. ParchLock brings useful tools alongside the Parchment interpreter, so you can focus on what happens next.

01

See where you've been

Local and World maps keep explored rooms in view. Discovered exit hints stay separate from routes you have travelled.

02

Keep your gear close

Equipment, contextual action menus and status use information from the game where supported. Available actions and statistics vary by story.

03

Leave yourself a clue

Add room notes, search known places and navigate along a known route. Word suggestions help with typing.

04

Make it comfortable

Adjust text and display settings, switch themes or listen with read aloud. Touch and desktop controls share the same adventure.

05

Take your progress with you

Use Tools for game saves, map packages and transcripts. Export files to your device and import them when you need them.

06

Build your own library

Manage compatible modules stored on this device. Imported stories remain local; you choose what to install and play.

A note on story support

Z-machine stories support the enhanced maps, items and action menus. Glulx stories are currently play-only. Game rules still apply: some inventory or status requests can use a turn.

Prefer typing? Useful commands
/note locked door, find a key;
Add a note to the current room.
/notes
Show notes for the current room.
/goto kitchen;
Prepare a route to a known room.
/see kitchen;
Browse a known room on the map.
/clear notes
Remove notes from the current room.
/clear map
Erase the map and start again from your current room.
