scenery object; its handler cs.zil ~6047-6053 answers WALK/LAMP-OFF/LAMP-ON/STOP with a message and never FSETs/FCLEARs
ONBIT -> a permanent "running" state, not player-switchable).

(2608-2629) and ALCHEMY-LAB (4718/4856) are rooms. Indirect setters (FSET/FCLEAR with a variable object): the 9 listed
above all set other flags (OPENBIT, NDESCBIT, LOCKED, SLIMEBIT, RMUNGBIT, PERSON, TOUCHBIT); none touches ONBIT.
Conclusion: the only player-switchable ONBIT item is the flashlight; one static non-room carrier (global waxer) is
always-on scenery.
